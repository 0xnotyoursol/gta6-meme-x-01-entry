# GTA 6 meme for make.fun

A GTA 6 meme, posted on X as an entry in make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X"
bounty. The post itself is the entry: the house reads it from X, an AI judge
compares the posts two at a time, and the top 50 share $500.

The meme: thirteen years of waiting for GTA 6, a Vice City sunset, and the
make.fun ghost waiting along with everyone else. `meme.png` is the picture
that goes up with the post.

`POST.json` holds the post's address. `main.ts` copies it to `/output`,
which is all the program has to do.
